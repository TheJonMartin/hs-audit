# HubSpot Audit: Install the Process Pro Audit App

## What

Process Pro will run a HubSpot audit of your portal. To do that without logging in to your account, you install our read-only audit app with one click, then complete a short checklist of items HubSpot cannot share automatically.

Why it matters: no screen-share to schedule, no key to copy or send, and the audit works from totals and percentages. Process Pro does not receive your contact names, emails or phone numbers.

What the app can and cannot do: it can only **read**. It cannot create, change or delete anything. The consent screen lists exactly what it can read, and you can uninstall it at any time to end access immediately.

## How

Time needed: about 5 minutes to install, 20 to 30 minutes for the checklist. The person installing must be able to install apps in your HubSpot account (usually a Super Admin).

1. **Open the install link** your Process Pro contact sent: `[INSTALL LINK]`.
2. **Choose the HubSpot account** to audit if asked.
3. **Review the permissions.** Every item should be a read permission. If you see anything that lets the app create, edit or delete data, stop and contact us.
4. **Click Connect app.** You will land on a confirmation page. Tell your Process Pro contact it is done.
5. **Fill in the intake form and `screenshot_checklist.md`** and send them back.
6. **When we confirm the audit is finished, uninstall the app.** Settings, then Integrations, then Connected Apps, then Process Pro Audit, then Uninstall. We will remind you.

## Done

You are done when all four are true:

- [ ] The app shows as connected in Settings, Integrations, Connected Apps.
- [ ] Your Process Pro contact has confirmed the connection worked.
- [ ] The intake form and screenshot checklist are sent back.
- [ ] After Process Pro confirms the audit has run, the app is uninstalled.
